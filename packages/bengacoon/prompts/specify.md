Load the `change-router` skill and follow it for this request.

The request:

$ARGUMENTS

The route is **specify**: what "done" looks like has not been settled. Announce
it and carry on.

Run `specify` first and stop at the list. Do not start the spine on unit one
until the list has been seen and agreed — a list built on the wrong understanding
costs a whole feature, and it is the cheapest thing in this flow to correct.

Then the spine runs once per unit, and the review gate faces the change as a
whole at the end, not once per unit.

If a unit's criterion cannot be written as something check-able, say which one
and why, and stop there rather than starting work on a wish.
